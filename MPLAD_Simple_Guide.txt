  PART 1 — WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This is a software system for managing the MPLADS scheme — which stands for
"Member of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme."

In simple words:
  Every Member of Parliament (MP) in India gets a fixed amount of money
  (currently ₹5 crore per year) to spend on small development works in
  their own constituency — like building roads, water tanks, schools, health
  centres, streetlights, etc.

The problem this software solves:
  Managing this money is complicated. There are strict rules about what the
  money can be used for, who can approve what, and how the work needs to be
  tracked. Without a proper system, money can be misused, works can go
  unfinished, or the same work can be approved twice.

What this software does:
  - It lets the MP formally recommend (propose) a new work.
  - It lets an Authority officer review and either APPROVE or REJECT the proposal.
  - It tracks the work from approval → in progress → completed → fully utilised.
  - It uses AI to automatically flag suspicious patterns (like money spent but
    no work done, or a project that has been delayed for years).
  - It lets the public see what works are happening in their area.
  - Every single action is recorded permanently and cannot be deleted or changed.

  PART 2 — WHO USES THIS SYSTEM?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

There are 4 types of users:

  1. MP (Member of Parliament)
     - The MP recommends which works should be done in their constituency.
     - They use the "MP Page" in the software.
     - They CANNOT approve their own proposals — that is done by someone else.

  2. Authority Officer
     - A government officer who reviews the MP's proposals.
     - They check if the proposal follows the rules (is it a community work?
       is there enough money left? has the same work already been done?).
     - They either SANCTION (approve) or REJECT the proposal.
     - If rejected, they must give a specific reason.

  3. Implementing Officer
     - Once a work is approved, an agency (like a state PWD department) actually
       builds it.
     - The implementing officer updates the system as the work progresses:
       Approved → In Progress → Completed → Fully Used

  4. Public / Citizens
     - Citizens can VIEW what works are happening in their area.
     - They can also UPLOAD photos or videos of works as evidence.
     - They cannot approve or change anything — they can only see and upload.

  PART 3 — WHAT ARE THE RULES? (What money can and cannot be used for)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ALLOWED works (community/public benefit):
  ✔  Bore wells, water tanks, pipelines (Drinking Water)
  ✔  School buildings, libraries, labs (Education)
  ✔  Health centres, toilets, sanitation (Healthcare & Sanitation)
  ✔  Streetlights, solar panels (Electricity / Clean Energy)
  ✔  Rural roads, culverts, bridges (Roads & Bridges)
  ✔  Playgrounds, gyms (Sports)
  ✔  Field channels, check dams (Irrigation)
  ✔  Community centres, parks (Urban Development)

NOT ALLOWED:
  ✘  Works that benefit only one individual (like building someone's house)
  ✘  Government office buildings or staff quarters
  ✘  Works in religious places (temples, mosques, churches, etc.)
  ✘  Statues or monuments
  ✘  Works costing less than ₹1 lakh
  ✘  Works outside the MP's own constituency
  ✘  Anything that is already covered by another government scheme

  PART 4 — HOW DOES A WORK GET DONE? (Step by step)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  STEP 1 — MP Submits a Proposal
    The MP goes to the "MP" page in the system and fills in a form:
      - What type of work? (e.g., Road construction)
      - Where? (e.g., Village Ramnagar, Dist. Varanasi)
      - How much will it cost? (e.g., ₹25,00,000)
    The AI immediately checks if this type of work is allowed and shows a
    green or red signal. The MP saves the proposal.

  STEP 2 — Authority Reviews the Proposal
    The Authority officer sees all pending proposals on their page.
    They check:
      - Is this work allowed under MPLADS rules?
      - Is there enough money remaining in the MP's fund for this year?
      - Has the same work already been done or approved nearby?
      - Is the cost reasonable compared to similar works?
    The AI helps with all these checks but the OFFICER makes the final decision.
    The officer either SANCTIONS or REJECTS the proposal.

  STEP 3 — If Sanctioned: A "Work" is Created
    Once approved, the system automatically creates a WORK record with a
    unique ID. The money is "committed" (reserved) from the MP's fund.
    An implementing agency (like a government department) is assigned.

  STEP 4 — Work Starts (In Execution)
    The implementing officer updates the system when work begins.
    They can also upload photos/videos from the site as evidence.

  STEP 5 — Work Finishes (Completed)
    When the physical work is done, the officer marks it Completed.
    They enter how much money was actually spent.

  STEP 6 — Fully Utilised
    After all payments are cleared, the work is marked Utilised.
    The fund is now officially "spent" for this work.

  At every single step, the system automatically records WHO did WHAT and WHEN.
  This record is permanent — nobody can delete or change it.

  PART 5 — WHAT DOES THE AI DO?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The AI is like an expert assistant that reads project data and highlights
anything suspicious. It NEVER makes a final decision — it just tells the
officer "hey, look at this carefully."

The AI does 4 things:

  1. ELIGIBILITY CHECK
     Looks at the type of work proposed and signals:
     - Green = This looks like a proper community benefit work.
     - Red   = This might be for individual benefit — not allowed.

  2. DUPLICATION CHECK
     Looks at existing assets and approved works to check if the same type
     of work has already been done or approved in the same area.
     - No duplicate = Fine.
     - Possible duplicate = Officer should verify.
     - Likely duplicate = Strong warning flag.

  3. COST CHECK
     Compares the proposed cost against thousands of real MPLADS projects.
     It adjusts for inflation (prices in 2018 vs 2024 are different) and
     also for location (building a road in Ladakh costs more than in UP).
     - Cost is normal = Fine.
     - Cost is too high = Warning (possible over-pricing).
     - Cost is suspiciously low = Warning (may be under-scoped or fake).

  4. ANOMALY & RISK DETECTION (on running works)
     Once a work is in progress, the AI monitors it and raises alerts like:
     - 🔴 CRITICAL: 80% of money is spent but only 10% of work is reported done.
     - 🔴 CRITICAL: The GPS location of uploaded photos is 8,000 metres away
                    from the actual project site — photos might be fake.
     - 🟠 HIGH: Project is 400 days delayed past its planned deadline.
     - 🟡 MEDIUM: No citizen reports uploaded for a project running 6+ months.
     - 🟢 LOW RISK: Everything looks normal.

  IMPORTANT: The AI is trained on data from 2,38,000+ real MPLADS projects
  from all over India. It uses machine learning — it has "learned" from past
  patterns of both normal projects and fraudulent/mismanaged ones.

  But remember: The AI just raises a flag. The human officer decides.

  PART 6 — HOW IS THE DATA STORED?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Think of it like a very organised filing cabinet with 9 folders:

  Folder 1 — Constituencies
    Stores a record for each constituency (MP's area).

  Folder 2 — Members (MPs)
    Stores a record for each MP.

  Folder 3 — Entitlement Years
    For each MP and each year, how much money do they have?
    How much is committed? How much is spent?

  Folder 4 — Public Asset Inventory
    A list of community assets that already exist in each area.
    (So we know what already exists before approving new works.)

  Folder 5 — Work Proposals
    Every proposal submitted by an MP, before it is approved or rejected.

  Folder 6 — Implementing Agencies
    A list of agencies/departments that can do the actual construction work.

  Folder 7 — Works
    Every approved and active work. Created ONLY after official approval.
    Contains the current status, money spent, completion status.

  Folder 8 — Work Evidence
    All photos and videos uploaded for any work.
    Each piece of evidence has a GPS location and timestamp.
    Once uploaded, it CANNOT be changed or deleted.

  Folder 9 — Action Audit
    A permanent log of every single action taken in the system.
    Who approved what, when, why was it rejected, etc.
    This is like a "black box" — completely tamper-proof.

  The data is stored in a simple database file (called "dev.db") on your
  computer. No internet or separate database server is needed.

  PART 7 — WHAT PAGES ARE IN THE WEB APP?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When you open the app in your browser (at http://localhost:3000), you will see:

  HOME PAGE (/)
    A public dashboard showing:
    - Total works across India, how much money is sanctioned and spent.
    - Breakdown by sector (how many road works, how many water works, etc.)
    - What types of works are allowed and not allowed.
    Anyone can see this page.

  MP PAGE (/mp)
    For the Member of Parliament to submit new work proposals.
    The AI shows a green/red signal for eligibility instantly.

  AUTHORITY PAGE (/authority)
    For the authority officer to see all pending proposals and approve or reject them.
    The AI's checks are shown here to help the officer make a decision.

  OFFICER PAGE (/officer)
    For the implementing agency to update the progress of works.
    Mark a work as "started", "completed", and enter how much was spent.

  ASSETS PAGE (/assets)
    Shows all existing public assets in each constituency.
    Helps avoid approving duplicate works.

  PUBLIC PAGE (/public)
    For citizens to:
    - See what works are happening in their area.
    - Upload a photo or video as evidence for any sanctioned work.

  MONITOR PAGE (/monitor)
    The AI monitoring dashboard.
    Officers can paste in project details and the AI analyses them instantly,
    showing all alerts with severity levels (Critical, High, Medium, Low).

  PART 8 — HOW TO START THE APPLICATION (Simple Steps)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

You only need to do Step 1-3 the FIRST TIME you set up the project.
After that, just do Step 4 and 5 every time you want to run it.

  STEP 1 — Open PowerShell (the black terminal window).

  STEP 2 — Type this command and press Enter:
      cd "C:\Users\nanth\OneDrive\Desktop\MPLAD\mplad-app"
    (This takes you into the web app's folder.)

  STEP 3 — Install the required software (first time only):
      npm install
    Wait for it to finish. You'll see some warnings — that's okay, ignore them.

  STEP 4 — Set up the database (first time only):
      npx prisma generate
      npx prisma db push

  STEP 5 — Start the web app:
      npm run dev
    You will see: "Ready in 944ms" and "Local: http://localhost:3000"
    This means it's running!

  STEP 6 — Open your browser and go to:
      http://localhost:3000
    You should see the MPLAD dashboard.

  TO STOP THE APP: Go back to PowerShell and press Ctrl + C.

  TO RUN THE AI SEPARATELY (optional, not required):
    Open a new PowerShell window and type:
      cd "C:\Users\nanth\OneDrive\Desktop\MPLAD\mplad_ai"
      node alert.js --demo
    This will print AI analysis for a sample project.

  PART 9 — PROBLEMS YOU MIGHT FACE (and what to do)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  PROBLEM 1: Python scripts (alert.py, train.py) don't run.
    What happens: An error saying "DLL blocked" or "Application Control policy."
    Why it happens: The computer is an institutional/school machine and Windows
                    is blocking certain Python files as a security measure.
    What to do: DON'T worry. Use the Node.js version instead:
                  node alert.js --demo
                The Node.js version does exactly the same thing and is not blocked.

  PROBLEM 2: "pip.exe failed to run" error.
    Why it happens: Same reason — the computer blocks pip (Python package installer).
    What to do: You don't need pip for this project. Node.js handles everything.

  PROBLEM 3: Yellow warning about "SWC compiler blocked."
    What you see in the terminal: A warning about "@next/swc-win32-x64-msvc"
    Why it happens: Same policy. Windows blocks the fast compiler.
    What to do: IGNORE IT. The app still works perfectly using a backup compiler.

  PROBLEM 4: "allow-scripts" warnings during npm install.
    What you see: Warnings about Prisma packages.
    What to do: IGNORE THEM. These are just warnings, not errors. Everything works.

  PROBLEM 5: The browser shows an error on first open.
    What to do: Make sure the terminal shows "Ready" before opening the browser.
                If the database isn't set up yet, run:
                  npx prisma generate
                  npx prisma db push
                Then refresh the browser.

  PART 11 — QUICK REFERENCE: KEY TERMS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Term                  What it means in plain English
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Proposal              A request by the MP to spend money on a particular work.
  Work                  An approved project that is actually being built/done.
  Sanctioned            Officially approved by the authority officer.
  In Execution          Work has started on the ground.
  Completed             Physical work is finished.
  Utilised              All payments are done, money is fully accounted for.
  Entitlement           The total fund available to an MP for a given year.
  Committed Amount      Money reserved for works that are approved but not yet done.
  Expended Amount       Money actually paid out for completed work.
  Action Audit          The permanent log of every decision made in the system.
  Work Evidence         Photos/videos uploaded from the work site as proof.
  GPS Coordinates       Exact location (latitude + longitude) recorded with evidence.
  Anomaly               Something unusual or suspicious detected by the AI.
  SMOTE                 A technique used to train the AI when fraud examples are rare.
  Ensemble Model        The AI uses two "brains" together (Random Forest + Gradient
                        Boosting) and combines their answers for better accuracy.
  Prisma                The tool that connects the web app to the database.
  SQLite                The database — a single file on your computer (dev.db).
  Next.js               The web framework used to build the website/app.
  Node.js               The software that runs JavaScript outside of a browser.
  npm run dev           The command that starts the web application.
